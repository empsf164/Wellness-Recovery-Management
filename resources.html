Core Principles

VERA Wellness Fundamentals

Four pillars that guide mindful, sustainable daily restoration.

1. Cadence Over Intensity

Small, repeatable daily habits yield greater long-term recovery than sporadic extreme interventions.

2. Downregulation Practice

Intentional shifts into parasympathetic states unlock cellular and muscular recovery faster.

3. Joint Decompression

Restoring articular range through gentle mobility relieves residual nervous system tension.

4. Observational Honesty

Recording subjective signals without judgment creates realistic feedback loops for better days.
