How VERA Access Security Works

1. Scoped Cryptographic Tokens

Share URLs contain randomly generated, single-recipient access tokens that only expose the specific data tables you explicitly ticked.

2. Enforced Expiration

Access automatically terminates after your chosen duration (24h, 7 days, 30 days) unless you choose to re-enable it.

3. Instant Client-Side Revoke

Clicking "Revoke" instantly terminates access. Subsequent visits to that URL will see an expired access notice.
