Answers & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding data privacy, sharing controls, and tracking mechanics.

Is VERA a medical diagnostic system?

No. VERA is strictly a personal wellness and recovery management platform for self-reflection and routine organization. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or clinical evaluations.

How does client access sharing work?

You can create encrypted, time-scoped sharing links with granular permissions. For example, you can authorize a coach to view routine consistency trends while keeping private journal notes completely hidden. You can revoke access at any second.

What export formats are supported?

You can export your complete records in printable, document-style PDF, tabular CSV (for spreadsheet software like Excel or Numbers), and structured machine-readable JSON archives.

Are my journal notes private?

Yes. Journal entries are strictly private by default. They are never included in shared professional links unless you explicitly check the permission box when creating an invitation.
